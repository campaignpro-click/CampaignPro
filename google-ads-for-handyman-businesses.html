Google Ads for handyman businesses

How handyman businesses use Google Ads to book more repair, mounting and assembly jobs on a small budget.

By CampaignPro · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer

Google Ads is one of the more affordable ways for a handyman business to get jobs. US averages are about $7.10 per click and $54 per lead, with a conversion rate around 13%.[1] The best results come from task-specific campaigns (mounting, assembly, repairs) alongside general "handyman" searches, a tight service area and clear pricing in the ads.

Does Google Ads work for handyman businesses?

Yes, and it's one of the more affordable home services to advertise. People searching "handyman near me" or "tv mounting service" usually have a specific job in mind and want it done soon. Google Ads puts you at the top for those searches in your area, and satisfied customers often come back with their next list of jobs.

Keywords to target and to block

Good handyman keywords

  • handyman near me
  • handyman services [city]
  • tv mounting service
  • drywall repair near me
  • furniture assembly service
  • small home repairs

Common negative keywords

  • jobs, hiring, salary, handyman license
  • diy, how to, youtube, tools
  • app, platform names (e.g. taskrabbit, thumbtack, angi)
  • home depot, lowes, hardware
  • free, cheap (test carefully)
  • services you can't legally do in your state (e.g. major electrical)

Local Services Ads vs. Search Ads for handyman businesses

Handyman businesses can also run Google Local Services Ads, which appear at the very top of results with a Google badge and charge per lead instead of per click. They can be a good source of small-job leads. Search Ads give you much more control over which services, keywords and areas you pay for. Many businesses get the most leads by running both.

Tips that make handyman ads convert

  • Name the tasks. Ads that mention TV mounting, drywall or furniture assembly convert better than a generic "handyman" message.
  • Show how pricing works: hourly rate, minimum charge or free estimates. Clear pricing wins the click.
  • Make booking easy: call button, text option and a short quote form with photo upload.
  • Mention trust signals: insured, background-checked, reviews, satisfaction guarantee.

Common handyman Google Ads mistakes

  • Paying for app and marketplace searches. People searching for a specific platform rarely hire an independent business.
  • Only bidding on "handyman". Task-specific keywords are often cheaper and closer to a booking.
  • Too big a service area. Small jobs aren't profitable after a long drive.
  • No conversion tracking. Without it, there's no way to see which tasks bring paying customers.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Google Ads cost for a handyman business?

US averages for handyman services are about $7.10 per click and $54 per lead, with a conversion rate around 13%, which makes it one of the more affordable home services to advertise.

Is Google Ads worth it for a handyman?

Usually yes, because searches are specific and ready to book and repeat customers are common. It works best with task-specific keywords, a tight service area and clear pricing in the ads.

What keywords should a handyman target?

Combine broad terms like "handyman near me" with specific tasks such as TV mounting, drywall repair, furniture assembly and door or deck repair, which often convert better.

What negative keywords should a handyman use?

Block job and license searches, DIY and tool searches, hardware store searches, marketplace app names, and any work you're not licensed to do in your state.

How much should a handyman spend on Google Ads?

At about $54 per lead on average, a budget of around $500 to $1,000 per month can bring a steady flow of jobs. Start focused on your most profitable tasks and area, then scale up.
