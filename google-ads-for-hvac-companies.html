Google Ads for HVAC companies

How HVAC contractors use Google Ads to get more repair, installation and maintenance calls, what it costs, and how to stop paying for clicks that never become jobs.

By CampaignPro · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer

Google Ads works well for HVAC companies because people search at the moment they need a repair or replacement. US averages are about $9.68 per click and $128 per lead for AC searches, and about $9.30 per click and $129 per lead for heating.[1] The best-performing accounts separate emergency repair, installation and maintenance into their own campaigns, block job-seeker and DIY searches, track phone calls and shift budget with the seasons.

Does Google Ads work for HVAC companies?

Yes, and it's one of the best-suited industries for it. When someone's air conditioning dies in July or the furnace stops in January, they search Google and call one of the first companies they see. Google Ads puts you in that spot for the searches you choose, in the areas you serve, at the hours you can take calls. Unlike most marketing, you can measure exactly which ads produced calls and booked jobs.

The catch is cost. HVAC clicks are among the more expensive in home services, so a poorly structured account burns money quickly on searches that were never going to become jobs.

How to structure an HVAC Google Ads account

The most common HVAC mistake is one campaign for everything. Emergency repairs, new installations and maintenance have different values, costs and urgency, so they need separate campaigns and budgets:

Keywords to target and to block

Good HVAC keywords

  • ac repair [city]
  • furnace repair near me
  • hvac company near me
  • ac installation cost
  • heat pump installation
  • emergency hvac service

Common negative keywords

  • jobs, salary, hiring, technician training
  • diy, how to, youtube, manual
  • parts, filter, thermostat (if you don't sell parts)
  • car ac, auto ac
  • free, cheap (test carefully)
  • window unit (if you don't service them)

Local Services Ads vs. Search Ads for HVAC

HVAC companies can also run Google Local Services Ads, which appear at the very top with the "Google Guaranteed" badge and charge per lead instead of per click. They're worth testing alongside Search Ads, but you get far less control over which services and searches you pay for. Many HVAC companies get the best results running both: Local Services Ads for volume and Search Ads for the specific high-value jobs they want more of.

Seasonality and budget

  • Shift budget with the weather. Push AC repair and installation in summer, heating in winter, and tune-ups in spring and fall.
  • Match ad hours to your phones. If nobody answers after 6pm, don't pay for evening emergency clicks, or make sure an answering service catches them.
  • Count phone calls properly. Most HVAC leads call. Track calls from ads and from your website, and only count calls long enough to be real inquiries.

Common HVAC Google Ads mistakes

  • Paying for job seekers and DIY searches. Without negative keywords, "hvac technician jobs" and "how to fix ac" can eat a large part of the budget.
  • Counting form views or page visits as leads. Google then optimizes for the wrong people.
  • One budget for every service. Cheap maintenance clicks crowd out the installation jobs that make the money.
  • Ads running outside the service area. Location settings left on "presence or interest" show ads to people who only searched about your area.
  • Sending every ad to the homepage. Someone searching "furnace repair" should land on a page about furnace repair, with your phone number at the top.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much should an HVAC company spend on Google Ads?

Start from the number of jobs you want and your cost per lead. US averages are around $128 per lead for AC and heating searches, so 10 leads a month would need roughly $1,300 in ad spend at average costs. Many HVAC companies start with a smaller test budget focused on their most profitable service and scale from there.

What is a good cost per lead for HVAC Google Ads?

US averages are about $128 per lead for air conditioning and $129 for heating searches. A good cost per lead is one that stays profitable after your close rate and job value: an installation worth several thousand dollars can justify a much higher cost per lead than a tune-up.

Should HVAC companies use Local Services Ads or Search Ads?

Often both. Local Services Ads charge per lead and show the Google Guaranteed badge at the top of results, but offer less control. Search Ads let you choose exactly which services, keywords and areas you pay for. Running both usually gives the most leads.

How long does it take for HVAC Google Ads to work?

Ads can start producing calls within days of launching. It usually takes the first few weeks to collect enough data to cut wasted searches and settle bids, and results keep improving with ongoing optimization.

What keywords should HVAC companies avoid?

Block searches from job seekers (jobs, salary, training), do-it-yourselfers (how to, diy, manual), parts shoppers, car AC searches and any services or equipment you don't offer.
