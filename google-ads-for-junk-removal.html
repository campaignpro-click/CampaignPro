Google Ads for junk removal companies

How junk removal and hauling companies use Google Ads to keep their trucks booked, and how to avoid paying for junk car and scrap searches.

By CampaignPro · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer

Google Ads works well for junk removal because searches are local and urgent. The best-performing accounts separate single-item jobs from high-value cleanouts, block junk car, scrap and free-pickup searches, target only the areas their trucks cover and track calls and texts as leads.

Does Google Ads work for junk removal companies?

Yes. People searching "junk removal near me" or "furniture removal" usually want it gone this week, often today. They call or request a quote from the first few companies that look reliable. Google Ads puts you there for the jobs you want, in the areas your trucks cover, and shows exactly which searches turned into booked jobs.

Keywords to target and to block

Good junk removal keywords

  • junk removal near me
  • junk hauling [city]
  • furniture removal service
  • estate cleanout company
  • appliance removal near me
  • same day junk removal

Common negative keywords

  • junk yard, salvage yard, scrap metal prices
  • junk car, sell my car, cash for cars
  • free junk pickup, city bulk pickup, landfill hours
  • donation pickup (if you don't offer it)
  • jobs, franchise, start a junk removal business
  • trash can, dumpster (if you don't rent them)

Junk car and scrap searches are the classic leak: they share the word "junk" but come from people selling a car, not paying for removal.

Local Services Ads vs. Search Ads for junk removal companies

Junk removal companies can also run Google Local Services Ads, which appear at the very top of results with a Google badge and charge per lead instead of per click. Junk removal is available as a Local Services Ads category in many areas. Search Ads give you much more control over which services, keywords and areas you pay for. Many businesses get the most leads by running both.

Tips that make junk removal ads convert

  • Show how pricing works. Pricing by truck load, a minimum charge or "free estimates" helps people choose you quickly.
  • Promote speed. Same-day and next-day availability is one of the strongest selling points in this industry.
  • Let people send photos. A text or upload option for a quick quote gets more responses than a long form.
  • Mention where it goes: donating and recycling items is a real reason customers choose one company over another.

Plan around busy seasons

Demand rises with spring cleaning and moving season, and around month-end when leases turn over. Increase budgets ahead of those periods and schedule ads for the days and hours your crews can take new jobs.

Common junk removal Google Ads mistakes

  • Paying for junk car and scrap searches. These can take a large share of clicks without a single removal job.
  • Advertising beyond your truck routes. Far-away jobs cost more in drive time than they earn.
  • Sending cleanout searches to the homepage. Estate and hoarder cleanouts need their own page that explains the process sensitively.
  • Not tracking calls and texts. Most junk removal leads call or text; count both.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Ads worth it for junk removal?

Usually yes, because junk removal searches are urgent and local. It works best with a tight service area, separate campaigns for cleanouts and single-item jobs, and negative keywords for junk car and scrap searches.

What keywords should a junk removal company target?

"Junk removal near me", "junk hauling [city]", and specific jobs like furniture, mattress and appliance removal, plus high-value cleanouts such as estate and garage cleanouts.

What negative keywords should junk removal companies use?

Block junk yard, salvage and scrap metal searches, junk car and cash-for-cars searches, free city pickup and landfill searches, and job or franchise searches.

Should junk removal companies use Local Services Ads?

Where the category is available, they're worth testing alongside Search Ads. Local Services Ads charge per lead; Search Ads give more control over job types and areas.

When is junk removal demand highest?

Demand usually rises with spring cleaning and summer moving season, and around the end of each month when people move out.
