Google Ads for landscaping companies

How landscaping and lawn care companies use Google Ads to win more design, hardscaping and maintenance clients, and how to plan campaigns around the seasons.

By CampaignPro · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer

Google Ads works for landscaping companies when budget goes to the most profitable services. US averages are about $8.76 per click and $118 per lead, with a conversion rate around 6.4%.[1] The best results come from separating lawn care, design and installation, and hardscaping, blocking idea and DIY searches, and switching seasonal campaigns on and off with demand.

Does Google Ads work for landscaping companies?

Yes, especially for higher-value work. Homeowners and property managers search for lawn care, landscape design and hardscaping when they're ready to get quotes, and Google Ads puts you in front of them in the neighborhoods you serve. The key is steering budget toward the services worth the most to you, because a patio or full landscape install is worth many times a single mow.

Keywords to target and to block

Good landscaping keywords

  • landscaping company [city]
  • lawn care service near me
  • patio installation
  • retaining wall contractor
  • landscape design service
  • yard cleanup service

Common negative keywords

  • jobs, hiring, salary, landscaper jobs
  • ideas, pictures, pinterest, diy
  • mulch, pavers, stone, supply, nursery (if you don't sell materials)
  • lawn mower, equipment, trimmer
  • landscape architect degree
  • free, cheap (test carefully)

Local Services Ads vs. Search Ads for landscapers

Landscapers can also run Google Local Services Ads, which appear at the very top of results with a Google badge and charge per lead instead of per click. They work well for lawn care and general landscaping leads. Search Ads give you much more control over which services, keywords and areas you pay for. Many businesses get the most leads by running both.

Plan around the seasons

  • Start before spring. Searches for landscaping and lawn care climb in early spring; campaigns should be running and tested before the rush.
  • Rotate seasonal campaigns. Spring cleanup, summer maintenance, fall leaf removal and winter snow removal each deserve their own on/off switch.
  • Book hardscaping early. Patio and wall projects are often planned months ahead, so advertise them before your calendar fills.

Common landscaping Google Ads mistakes

  • Paying for idea and DIY searches. "backyard landscaping ideas" gets huge volume but rarely hires anyone.
  • Mixing mowing and big projects. Low-value mowing clicks use up budget meant for installs and hardscaping.
  • Advertising outside the route. Leads outside your service area waste crew time or go unanswered.
  • No photos or proof. Landscaping is visual; ads and landing pages without real project photos convert poorly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does Google Ads cost for landscapers?

US averages for landscaping are about $8.76 per click and $118 per lead, with a conversion rate around 6.4%. Hardscaping and design leads often cost more but are worth far more per job.

Is Google Ads worth it for a landscaping company?

Yes, when budget is focused on profitable services and your real service area. It works best for design, installation and hardscaping projects, and for recurring lawn care clients with good lifetime value.

When should landscapers start advertising?

Before demand peaks in early spring, so campaigns are tested by the time the rush starts. Seasonal services such as leaf and snow removal should switch on and off with the seasons.

What negative keywords should landscaping companies use?

Block job searches, idea and DIY searches (ideas, pictures, pinterest), materials and equipment searches if you don't sell them, and education-related searches.

Should lawn care and landscape installation be separate campaigns?

Yes. They attract different customers with very different job values, so separate campaigns stop cheap mowing clicks from using the budget meant for bigger projects.
