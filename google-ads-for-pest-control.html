Google Ads for pest control companies

How pest control companies use Google Ads to win more termite, rodent, bed bug and recurring-plan customers.

By CampaignPro · Updated September 26, 2026

Short answer

Google Ads works well for pest control because pest problems are urgent and many customers stay on recurring treatment plans. The best-performing accounts split campaigns by pest (general, termites, rodents, bed bugs), block DIY and product searches, plan budgets around pest seasons and track phone calls as conversions.

Does Google Ads work for pest control companies?

Yes. When someone finds termites, mice or bed bugs, they want a professional quickly, and they search Google to find one. Google Ads puts your company at the top for those searches in your service area. And because many customers sign up for recurring treatment plans, a single new customer from an ad can be worth years of revenue.

Split campaigns by pest

Different pests mean different urgency, job values and seasons. Separate campaigns let you put budget where the profit is:

Keywords to target and to block

Good pest control keywords

  • pest control near me
  • exterminator [city]
  • termite treatment cost
  • bed bug exterminator
  • mouse exterminator near me
  • quarterly pest control service

Common negative keywords

  • diy, spray, home remedy, natural
  • home depot, lowes, amazon, product names
  • jobs, technician salary, pest control license
  • pictures, identification, "what does a termite look like"
  • free inspection (test carefully)
  • pests or services you don't treat

"How to get rid of…" searches are a judgment call: some people end up hiring a professional, but many just want a product. Test them in a separate, lower-budget campaign rather than mixing them with high-intent searches.

Local Services Ads vs. Search Ads for pest control companies

Pest control companies can also run Google Local Services Ads, which appear at the very top of results with a Google badge and charge per lead instead of per click. Pest control is one of the core Local Services Ads categories. Search Ads give you much more control over which services, keywords and areas you pay for. Many businesses get the most leads by running both.

Plan around pest seasons

  • Spring and summer bring the highest overall demand, plus termite swarm season and mosquitoes.
  • Fall and winter bring rodent calls as mice and rats move indoors.
  • Bed bugs are a year-round, urgent category that often justifies its own steady budget.

Common pest control Google Ads mistakes

  • Paying for DIY and product shoppers. Sprays, traps and home remedies attract clicks from people who want to do it themselves.
  • Treating every pest the same. Termite and bed bug jobs are worth much more than a one-time ant call and deserve their own budgets.
  • Not promoting recurring plans. Ads and landing pages should make it easy to sign up for ongoing service, where the long-term value is.
  • No call tracking. Most pest control customers call; untracked calls make good campaigns look bad.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Google Ads worth it for pest control companies?

Usually yes. Pest searches are urgent, and customers who sign up for recurring treatment plans are worth far more than their first visit. Results are best when campaigns are split by pest, DIY searches are blocked and calls are tracked.

What keywords should a pest control company target?

Service-plus-location searches like "pest control near me" and "exterminator [city]", plus pest-specific searches for termites, rodents and bed bugs, which are often worth more per job.

What negative keywords should pest control companies use?

Block DIY and home remedy searches, retail and product searches, job and license searches, pest identification searches and any pests or services you don't treat.

When is the busiest season for pest control advertising?

Overall demand peaks in spring and summer, with termite swarms and mosquitoes. Rodent searches rise in fall and winter, and bed bug searches stay urgent year-round.

Should pest control companies use Local Services Ads?

They're worth running alongside Search Ads. Local Services Ads charge per lead and show a Google badge at the top of results; Search Ads give you control over which pests, services and areas you pay for.
