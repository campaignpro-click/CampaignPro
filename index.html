Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Everything is month-to-month. There are no 6 or 12 month lock-ins. You stay because it's working.

There is no fixed minimum. You can start testing with as little as $500 per month in ad spend and scale once you see which campaigns bring leads.

I've never run Google Ads. Can you help?

Yes. New advertisers get the account setup for free: campaign structure, keywords, ads and conversion tracking, built properly from day one.

We talk about your business, your goals and your current marketing. If you already run Google Ads, you get a free audit of your account showing where budget is being wasted. New campaigns usually start generating leads within the first few weeks. The bigger gains come from ongoing optimization: across client accounts, cost per lead has dropped by 27% on average within the first 90 days.
