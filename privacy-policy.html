Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

CampaignPro ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

Information We Collect

When you book a call through our Calendly scheduling tool, we collect the information you provide, such as your name and email address. When you request a free audit through the form on our website, we collect your name, email address, phone number and, if you provide them, your website address and a description of your advertising situation, together with information about the ad or link that brought you to the page (such as the campaign name). We do not collect payment information through this site.

How We Use It

We use this information solely to schedule and prepare for your consultation call or audit, and to follow up regarding our services. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This site uses Calendly for scheduling. Calendly has its own privacy policy governing the data you submit through their widget, available at calendly.com/privacy.

Audit requests submitted through our website form are stored securely in Google Sheets (Google Workspace), which is accessible only to CampaignPro. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use this website, for example which sections are read and where visitors click or scroll. Clarity collects usage data through first- and third-party cookies and similar technologies, and may record anonymized session replays and heatmaps. Clarity does not capture the details you enter in the Calendly booking form, which is handled by Calendly. We use this data only to improve the website. For more information about how Microsoft collects and uses data, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

We also use Google Analytics 4 to measure website traffic and to understand which marketing channels lead to booked calls. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages visited, approximate location, device type and how you arrived at the site. We do not send your name, email address or other booking details to Google Analytics. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

On pages we use for advertising, we may use the Meta Pixel to measure how our Facebook and Instagram ads perform, for example how many visitors requested an audit after clicking an ad. The Meta Pixel uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your visit and may share it with Meta Platforms, Inc. We do not send your name or email address to Meta. You can manage how Meta uses this information in your Facebook ad preferences. See the Meta Privacy Policy.

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete any information we hold about you by contacting us directly through our booking link.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our booking page.
